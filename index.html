Engineered for Pro Creative Workflow

Built specifically for artists, animators, and sculptors

Every layer of Penpal was built from scratch in Swift 6 for zero latency, zero frame drops, and bulletproof stability.

⚡

240Hz Coalesced Touch Engine

Bypasses standard UIKit event smoothing. Extracts Apple Pencil high-frequency sub-frame samples directly from event.coalescedTouches for glass-smooth curve interpolation without jagged edges.

🎯

16-Bit CoreGraphics Tablet Injection

Synthesizes true kCGEventMouseSubtypeTabletPoint events to kCGHIDEventTap. Maps altitude/azimuth spherical angles into Cartesian degrees for tilt shading in Photoshop and Blender.

🖐️

Trackpad Pan, Zoom & Rotate

Two fingers instantly navigate your desktop canvas. Smooth continuous pixel scroll (CGEventCreateScrollWheelEvent2) and trackpad pinch magnification keep your flow uninterrupted.

🛡️

Hardware-Enforced Palm Rejection

Single-finger touches are 100% inert. While your Apple Pencil is in hover or drawing contact, finger recognizers are completely suppressed at the hardware level—zero stray marks or cursor jumps.

🎛️

Quick Action Dock & Studio Themes

One-tap Undo (Cmd+Z), Redo (Cmd+Shift+Z), and Eraser (E) right at your fingertips. Switch between 5 OLED and paper canvas themes with left- or right-hand dock positioning.

🦺

Emergency Disconnect Fail-Safe

If your USB-C cable gets yanked out mid-stroke, Penpal immediately synthesizes an emergency kCGEventLeftMouseUp, completely preventing stuck mouse drag loops on your Mac desktop.

System Architecture

How Penpal Achieves < 3ms Latency

Zero third-party frameworks. Pure Swift from the iPad touch digitizer to the macOS HID event tap.

1. Capture

iPad Pro Canvas

UIResponder collects 240Hz coalesced Pencil touches, pressure, altitude & azimuth angles.

→
2. Pack

40B Binary Frame

Contiguous @frozen BitwiseCopyable packet with zero memory allocations or heap copying.

→
3. Transport

usbmuxd Socket

Sub-millisecond USB domain socket bridge over /var/run/usbmuxd (Port 8124, TCP_NODELAY).

→
4. Inject

macOS HID Tap

EventInjector maps 4:3 pillarbox coordinates & synthesizes native tablet events into Photoshop/CSP.

Wire Protocol Memory Layout

Zero Allocation • Fixed Stride

StylusPacket (40 Bytes)

00..01 Magic (0x504E) Sync header identifier
02..03 Version & Opcode v1.0 (0x01), Opcode 0x01 (Stylus)
04..07 Sequence & Reserved Drop/loss detection
08..15 Timestamp (Double) Mach continuous time
16..23 Norm X & Y (Float32) Normalized tablet coordinates (0.0–1.0)
24..27 Pressure (Float32) Continuous 0.0–1.0 (scales to 0–65535)
28..35 Tilt X & Y (Float32) Cartesian tilt angles (-60.0° to +60.0°)
36..39 Phase & Padding Hover / Began / Moved / Ended + 8B alignment

GesturePacket (24 Bytes)

00..03 Magic, Ver, Opcode 0x504E, v1, Opcode 0x02 (Gesture)
04..07 Sequence & Reserved Sequence counter
08..15 Timestamp (Double) Mach continuous time
16..19 Delta X & Y (Int16) Pixel scroll deltas (Continuous Pan)
20..23 Scale & Rotation (Int16) Pinch magnification & rotation angles
Quick Start

How to Install and Use Penpal

Set up the macOS host daemon and deploy the iPad client app in just 5 minutes.

1

Clone Repository & Launch macOS Companion App

PenpalKit builds with Swift 6 strict concurrency checking. Launch the native macOS companion monitor window (or headless daemon) via Swift Package Manager:

Terminal (macOS) 
$ git clone https://github.com/Renoceros/Penpal.git
$ cd Penpal
$ swift run --package-path Packages/PenpalKit PenpalCompanion

Or run headless CLI daemon: swift run --package-path Packages/PenpalKit PenpalHostSpike

2

Grant macOS Accessibility Permissions

CoreGraphics requires Accessibility authorization to inject tablet cursor and pressure events into third-party creative apps:

  1. Open System Settings on your Mac.
  2. Navigate to Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  3. Click the + button (or toggle on) and add Terminal (or your compiled host app binary).
3

Deploy the iPad App via Xcode

You can deploy Penpal onto your personal iPad using a standard free Apple Developer account:

Terminal (macOS) 
$ open Penpal.xcodeproj
  1. Plug your iPad into your Mac with a USB-C cable.
  2. Select the Penpal scheme and your iPad in the device menu.
  3. Under Signing & Capabilities, select your personal Team.
  4. Press Cmd + R to build and run the app onto your iPad.
  5. (First time only) On your iPad, open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and tap Trust.
4

Plug In & Start Drawing!

Keep your iPad connected via USB. Launch Penpal on your iPad and run the PenpalHost on your Mac:

  • ✓ The host automatically connects to the iPad on port 8124 over usbmuxd.
  • ✓ Open Photoshop, Clip Studio Paint, or Blender.
  • ✓ Hover your Apple Pencil above the iPad to preview cursor position, and press down to paint with 16-bit pressure!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Penpal's design, compatibility, and performance.

Transmitting a 120Hz 4K screen image requires heavy video encoding (H.264/HEVC), network buffering, and GPU decoding on the iPad. This introduces 25–60ms of visual lag, heats up your iPad, drains battery quickly, and causes pen strokes to feel disconnected from your hand.

By operating as a pure digitizer (identical to professional Wacom Intuos tablets), your iPad displays no video. You look at your calibrated desktop monitor while your hand glides smoothly over the iPad surface—giving you instant, sub-3ms tactile response without heat or battery degradation.

Sidecar and Astropad must capture macOS desktop frames, encode them to video streams, packetize them, stream them over network layers, and wait for the iPad to decode and display each frame.

Penpal bypasses the entire video pipeline. It captures raw hardware touch coordinates at 240Hz, serializes them into tiny 40-byte binary packets, and pumps them over a raw USB Unix domain socket (/var/run/usbmuxd). Total serialization, USB transit, and event injection take less than 3 milliseconds.

Penpal uses a hardware-enforced priority lockout:

  • Single-finger touches are 100% inert and never generate cursor clicks or strokes.
  • The moment your Apple Pencil hovers over the screen or makes contact (pressure > 0), all multi-touch recognizers are completely disabled.
  • Resting your entire palm or side of your hand on the iPad screen will never cause accidental marks, tool switching, or cursor jumping.

Penpal includes an automated fail-safe. If the USB socket experiences an unexpected EOF or disconnection while you are mid-stroke, EventInjector immediately synthesizes an emergency kCGEventLeftMouseUp event. This prevents your macOS desktop from getting stuck in mouse-drag mode.
