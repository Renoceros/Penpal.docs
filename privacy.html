Penpal Privacy Policy

Penpal is built on an uncompromising privacy foundation: we do not collect, store, track, sell, or transmit your personal data. Everything you draw, tap, or configure stays 100% on your personal hardware.

Summary in Plain English: Penpal is an offline, zero-video stylus digitizer tool. We don't have user accounts, cloud databases, tracking cookies, or analytics SDKs. Stylus pressure, coordinates, and gestures are transmitted over your local physical USB cable or direct peer-to-peer connection directly into macOS event taps and discarded immediately from RAM.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Penpal is engineered specifically to avoid collecting any user information. We believe software utility should not come at the expense of privacy.

  • No Personal Identification: We never ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, account credentials, or physical addresses.
  • No Screen or Artwork Content: Penpal is a zero-video digitizer. The app does not capture your screen, read your canvas pixels, record camera input, or record your artwork.
  • No Audio or Microphone Data: We do not access or record audio.
  • No Keystroke Logging: Penpal does not log keystrokes. Quick action buttons and pencil shortcuts (e.g., Undo, Eraser) only trigger specific mapped commands on your paired Mac.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: We do not include third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or Flurry). No telemetry is sent to us or any third party.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: Penpal contains zero ads, zero ad-network SDKs, and does not request or track Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).

2. Data Handled Locally on Your Devices

To deliver sub-3ms digitizer responsiveness, Penpal processes raw input signals locally in real-time:

  • Real-Time Stylus Packets: When using an Apple Pencil, the iPad app samples input at 240Hz (X/Y coordinates normalized to 0.0–1.0, 16-bit pressure 0–65535, tilt altitude and azimuth angles, button flags, and timestamps). These binary packets (40 bytes each) are streamed directly across the local socket buffer to the Penpal companion app on your Mac.
  • Ephemeral In-Memory Processing: Input packets are converted into native macOS CGEvent tablet point events in real time. They are never written to disk, logged to permanent storage, or cached beyond the instantaneous socket ring buffer.
  • Local App Preferences: Non-sensitive UI preferences (such as canvas theme selection, corner crop marks toggle, display aspect ratio mapping, and dock positioning) are saved locally on your device via standard UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

3. Network Communications & USB Bridge

Penpal communicates between your iPad and your Mac using local protocols:

  • Physical USB (usbmuxd): By default, the app uses a direct physical Lightning or USB-C cable connected to your Mac via Apple's native usbmuxd protocol on local port 8124. All traffic is confined to the physical wire between your two devices.
  • Local Network / Wi-Fi: If configured over local Wi-Fi or Apple Wireless Direct Link (AWDL), communication is strictly point-to-point across your local subnet. Penpal never connects to external servers, cloud proxies, or remote relays.

Zero Cloud Dependency: Penpal works completely offline without an active internet connection. You can disconnect your router or turn off internet access and Penpal will function with full capability.

4. Third-Party Libraries and SDKs

Penpal does not bundle any closed-source third-party tracking, profiling, or advertising libraries. All core logic is written in native Swift using Apple system frameworks (UIKit, SwiftUI, CoreGraphics, IOKit, Network).

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Penpal does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Because our software collects no personal information whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages.

6. Compliance with Global Privacy Laws (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • We do not act as a data controller or data processor of personal information because zero personal data is gathered.
  • We do not "sell" or "share" personal information under CCPA/CPRA definitions.
  • Because no personal data is collected or stored, requests for data access, porting, or deletion are inherently satisfied by design (zero records exist).

7. Architecture & Privacy Verification

Penpal is engineered to operate strictly offline. You can verify through standard system inspection tools (such as macOS Network Monitor, Little Snitch, or Wireshark) that no telemetry, background pings, or analytics packets are ever dispatched from either the iOS application or the macOS companion.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update or modify this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted directly to this URL with an updated revision date. Any changes will continue to adhere to our core principle of preserving zero user data collection.
